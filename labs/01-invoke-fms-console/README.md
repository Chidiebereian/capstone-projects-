**Track:** Foundation Models · **Mode:** Console · **Services:** Amazon Bedrock

## Objective
Used the Bedrock text and image playgrounds to compare foundation models from several providers and tune inference parameters.

## What I did

- Tuned temperature, top-p and max tokens for determinism vs creativity
